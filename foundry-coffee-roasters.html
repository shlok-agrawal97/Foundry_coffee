The process

From bean to cup

Scroll or use the arrows below — five stages, same order every batch runs through.

01 / Sourcing

Sourcing

We buy direct from four cooperatives, paying above fair-trade floor for lots we've cupped ourselves.

02 / Roasting

Roasting

Small drum batches of 12kg, tracked minute-by-minute against a roast curve unique to each origin.

03 / Cooling

Cooling

Beans drop into the cooling tray the instant they hit target, locking the roast at exactly the right second.

04 / Cupping

Cupping

Every batch is cupped blind against the reference roast before it's cleared for packing.

05 / Packing

Packing

Bagged and valved within an hour of cooling, dated by hand, shipped within the week.
